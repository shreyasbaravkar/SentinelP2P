Engineering Layer 01

Synthetic Data & Fraud Injection Pipeline

To train and evaluate the detection pipeline without compromising sensitive enterprise data, SentinelP2P includes plant_fraud.py. This module populates realistic vendor databases, employee directories, and transaction logs, subsequently injecting 8 distinct procurement fraud topologies.

The script assigns ground-truth fraud labels to each transaction record, enabling rigorous supervised and semi-supervised validation of both GNN models and rule-based Cypher queries.

plant_fraud.py - Injection Module Snippet Python 3.10+ 
def inject_shell_vendor_scheme(df_vendors, df_employees):
    """Injects shared contact info between employees and shell vendors."""
    compromised_emps = df_employees.sample(n=5)
    df_vendors.loc[0:4, 'contact_phone'] = compromised_emps['phone'].values
    df_vendors.loc[0:4, 'contact_email'] = compromised_emps['email'].values
    df_vendors.loc[0:4, 'is_shell_vendor'] = True
    return df_vendors, compromised_emps['employee_id'].tolist()

Engineering Layer 02

Graph Database & Deterministic Rules

Enterprise procurement is naturally relational. SentinelP2P constructs a property graph in Neo4j connecting Employee, Vendor, PurchaseOrder, Invoice, and Contract nodes.

Deterministic compliance checks are executed via optimized Cypher queries. These rules instantly flag structural policy violations, such as an employee approving their own purchase order or multiple POs split just below authorization thresholds.

Simulated Enterprise Threats

Comprehensive 8-Scheme Fraud Taxonomy

Detailed analysis of all eight real-world procurement fraud schemes implanted in the test bed and targeted by our detection engines.

F1
SHELL VENDOR

Shared Contact Information

A fictitious vendor entity shares direct personal telephone numbers, bank accounts, or physical mailing addresses with active internal corporate employees.

Detection: Neo4j Cypher Rule
F2
SPLIT PURCHASE ORDERS

Threshold Evasion

Large purchase orders are intentionally divided into multiple smaller transactions executed on the same day to bypass management authorization thresholds.

Detection: Neo4j Cypher Rule
F3
PHANTOM SHIPMENTS

Non-Delivery Fraud

Invoices are submitted and fully paid for goods, raw materials, or consulting services with zero corresponding warehouse receiving logs or inventory entries.

Detection: Heterogeneous GNN
F4
SEGREGATION OF DUTIES

Duty Breaches

The exact same employee profile acts as both the requisition creator who raises the purchase order and the approver who authorizes disbursement.

Detection: Neo4j Cypher Rule
F5
DUPLICATE INVOICING

Double Billing Scheme

Identical invoice numbers, exact line-item amounts, or slightly altered invoice descriptions submitted across separate accounting periods for single fulfillment.

Detection: Vector RAG Match
F6
RATE MISMATCH

Contract Breaches

Invoiced unit prices or hourly billing rates exceed the officially signed master service agreement terms by significant percentage margins.

Detection: Vector RAG Match
F7
GHOST EMPLOYEES

Payroll Padding

Terminated, inactive, or completely fabricated personnel records receiving ongoing salary allocations or direct procurement payouts.

Detection: Heterogeneous GNN
F8
BID RIGGING

Collusive Bidding Rings

Multiple competing vendor entities systematically rotate winning bids while sharing identical IP addresses, bank routing details, or submission metadata.

Detection: Heterogeneous GNN
Experimental Evaluation & Metrics

Zero-Shot Generalization Test (Scheme F6)

Evaluating model robustness and structural discovery against unseen price inflation patterns.

To rigorously test how effectively the heterogeneous graph neural network handles novel variants of financial corruption, scheme F6 (Rate Mismatch) was intentionally withheld from the model during initial supervised training.

Despite having zero prior exposure to explicit rate mismatch signatures in training labels, the structural message-passing architecture successfully flagged topological anomalies and anomalous vendor clustering with high confidence.
