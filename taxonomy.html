The 8 Implanted Fraud Schemes

Targeted patterns tested across graph queries, GNN networks, and vector RAG indices.

F1
SHELL VENDOR

Shared Contact Info

Vendor shares direct personal phone numbers or email addresses with active corporate employees.

Target: Neo4j Cypher Rule Check
F2
SPLIT POs

Threshold Evasion

Purchase orders split into multiple transactions under approval limits on the same day to bypass reviews.

Target: Neo4j Cypher Rule Check
F3
PHANTOM SHIPMENTS

Non-Delivery

Invoices paid in full for goods or services with zero matching warehouse or receiving receipts.

Target: Heterogeneous GNN
F4
SEGREGATION OF DUTIES

Duty Violations

The exact same employee both raises (creates) and approves the purchase order.

Target: Neo4j Cypher Rule Check
F5
DUPLICATE INVOICING

Double Billing

Identical invoice numbers or line items submitted across separate billing cycles.

Target: Vector RAG Semantic Match
F6
RATE MISMATCH

Contract Breaches

Invoice amounts exceeding agreed master service contract rates by more than 30%.

Target: Vector RAG Semantic Match
F7
GHOST EMPLOYEES

Payroll Padding

Inactive or terminated staff records receiving direct vendor payouts or salary allocations.

Target: Heterogeneous GNN
F8
BID RIGGING

Collusive Bidding

Multiple vendors rotating winning bids while sharing identical metadata or submission channels.

Target: Heterogeneous GNN

Detection Pipeline Mapping

How each scheme category is isolated across the multi-modal detection core.

Cypher Rule Queries

Handles deterministic structural policies such as F1, F2, and F4 where explicit database relationship rules define violations.

Heterogeneous GNNs

Uncovers complex relational anomalies like F3, F7, and F8 through message-passing across multi-typed enterprise network topologies.

Vector RAG Semantic Match

Catches unstructured text discrepancies such as F5 and F6 by matching invoice line-items against master contracts via Qdrant.
